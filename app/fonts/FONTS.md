# Fonts used by Neon Shore

The cover-art studio ships three display fonts. All three are released under the
SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1 — the full text of the licence lives at
https://scripts.sil.org/OFL.

- **Anton** — Regular 400. The heavy condensed sans used for big cover captions.
- **Pacifico** — Regular 400. The casual script used for handwritten-style accents.
- **Playfair Display** — Bold 700. The high-contrast serif used for elegant titles
  and the gold-foil title treatment.

The actual font binaries are the `.woff2` files sitting next to this note in
`app/fonts/`. No other font files are bundled with the app.
